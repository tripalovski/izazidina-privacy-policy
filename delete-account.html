Delete Your IzaZidina Account

Last updated: November 21, 2026

IzaZidina, developed by IzaZidina, lets you permanently delete your account and associated data. You do not need to keep the app installed to request deletion — see the options below.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (immediate)

  1. Open IzaZidina and sign in.
  2. Go to Account.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

This takes effect immediately — there is no waiting period, and it cannot be undone.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email (no app required)

Email iza.zidina@gmail.com from the address associated with your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We will confirm your identity and complete the deletion, normally within a few business days.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your account credentials (email, authentication record)
  • Your profile (display name, avatar)
  • All territories and claims you own
  • Your friend connections and friend requests
  • Your coin balance, purchases, and active boosts
  • Your notifications and push-notification registration
  • Your trust/anti-cheat status and gameplay history tied to your account
