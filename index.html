IzaZidina Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

IzaZidina ("we", "us", "the app") is a location-based territory game for mobile devices. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using IzaZidina, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account information

To use the app you create an account with an email address and password. We store your email address and a hashed password (via our backend provider, Supabase) in order to authenticate you and let you sign in across sessions. Your email address may be shown to you on your own leaderboard row; other players only ever see an anonymized player ID.

1.2 Location data

IzaZidina's core gameplay requires your device's GPS location. While you are actively recording a run, the app reads your location to build the path you trace on the map and to submit territory claims. Location is used only while you are using the app and recording a run; we do not track your location in the background or when the app is closed.

1.3 Motion / step-sensor data

The app reads your device's step counter / motion sensor while you are recording a run. This data is used solely as an anti-cheat signal to confirm that a claimed run corresponds to real physical movement. We do not use this data for health purposes, and we do not share it with health or fitness platforms.

1.4 Gameplay data

We store the territory claims, runs, and scores associated with your account (e.g., paths you submit, territory you own, your mass/leaderboard score) so the game can function and so you can see your own history and standing.

1.5 Push notification tokens

If you enable notifications, the app registers a device token with Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) so we can send you gameplay notifications (for example, when your territory is challenged). We do not use this token for advertising.

1.6 Information we do not collect

We do not collect contacts, photos, browsing history, or precise location when the app is not in active use. We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not sell your data.

3. How We Store and Share Information

Your data is stored with our backend provider, Supabase, which hosts our database and authentication. Map tiles are served through our own backend and a map-tile provider used only to render the base map (no personal data is sent to the map-tile provider beyond the tile coordinates needed to draw the map you are viewing). Push notifications are delivered through Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google).

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with advertisers or data brokers. We may disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights, or to investigate fraud and cheating.

4. Data Retention

We retain your account and gameplay data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal information (email address) within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain records by law or to prevent abuse.

5. Your Choices and Rights

  • Location and motion permissions: you can deny or revoke these permissions in your device settings; doing so will prevent you from recording runs, but you can still view the map and leaderboard.
  • Notifications: you can disable push notifications at any time in your device settings.
  • Account deletion: you may request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time by contacting us at the email below.
  • Access / correction: you may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, by contacting us.

6. Children's Privacy

IzaZidina is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures (including encrypted storage of credentials and secure transport) to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. International Users

IzaZidina is operated from Serbia. If you use the app from another country, your information will be processed on servers that may be located outside your country of residence.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this same URL with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
