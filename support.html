Frequently Asked Questions

The app can't find my location / "Finish run" fails

IzaZidina needs GPS access while you're recording a run. Check that Location Services are enabled for IzaZidina in your device's Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services, and that permission is set to "While Using the App" or "Always". Weak GPS signal indoors or near tall buildings can also prevent a run from closing correctly — try again outdoors with a clear view of the sky.

My run was rejected or flagged

Runs are checked against your device's motion sensors to confirm real movement. If a genuine run was rejected, make sure motion/fitness permissions are enabled for the app in Settings → Privacy & Security → Motion & Fitness, and contact us with the approximate time of the run so we can look into it.

I'm not receiving notifications

Enable notifications for IzaZidina in Settings → Notifications → IzaZidina. If they're already enabled and you still don't receive them, try signing out and back in, or contact support.

How do I delete my account or data?

You can delete your account directly in the app (Account → Delete Account), or follow the steps on our account deletion page, which also lists exactly what data is removed.

I found a bug or have feedback

We'd love to hear it. Email iza.zidina@gmail.com with a description of what happened, your device model, and iOS version if possible — screenshots help a lot.
